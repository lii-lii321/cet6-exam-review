一、考情速览（先花 3 分钟搞清敌人）

项目 内容
题型 给首句评论型：题目给你一句英文开头句（加引号），33 套真题无一例外
硬约束 首句必须一字不差照抄；词数 150–200（2023.12 起注明不含所给句）
时间 30 分钟（建议：审题 3 分钟 → 写作 22 分钟 → 检查 5 分钟）
文体 三段式议论/评述文

二、万能三段框架（背这一张图就够）

骨架

P1 抄首句 + 搭桥句（2–3 句）        【为什么重要 / 我怎么看】
P2 理由① + 展开 + 举例 + 理由② + 收束  【To begin with → Take... → Moreover → As a result】
P3 结论重申 + 升华 + 行动倡议        【In conclusion → far more than... → Only by... can we...】

P1 搭桥：抄完首句立刻接一句，二选一

A. 背景延伸型（话题 → 时代背景 → 重要性）

  • As knowledge updates at an unprecedented pace, the skills we master at school can no longer carry us through an entire career, which makes the capacity to learn on our own a decisive advantage.（2024.6）
  • In an age flooded with short videos and endless distractions, the ability to control oneself has become the very foundation of academic success and character building.（2024.12）
  • Not long ago, learning was confined to a fixed classroom and a single textbook, but technology has broken these limits and reshaped how we acquire knowledge.（2022.12）

B. 亮明态度型（这不是…而是…）

  • This trend, in my view, is not a passing fashion but a wise and even necessary response to the age we live in.（2022.6）
  • This statement reveals a simple truth: ambition alone means little unless it is anchored to attainable targets and sustained effort.（2024.12）
  • This awareness is well founded, for in an age reshaped by technology, the ability to handle digital tools has become as basic as reading and writing.（2022.9）

P2 主体：万能四步

  1. 起理由：To begin with, ... / For one thing, ... / The reason is straightforward: ...
  2. 举例：Take my own experience: ... / Take sb as an example. / A case in point is ...
  3. 递进第二点：Moreover, ... / What is more, ...
  4. 收束：As a result, ... / That experience taught me that ...

P3 结论：万能三连

重申（In conclusion, ... is far more than ...; it is ...）→ 升华（比喻句）→ 倡议（Therefore, we should ... / Only by ... can we ...）

🔑 考场救急模板（任何话题都能套，把【】换成题目关键词）： In conclusion, 【主题】 is far more than 【表面价值】; it is 【升华比喻】. Therefore, we should consciously 【行动】 and treat 【每一天/每次机会】 as a chance to 【主题动作】. Only by doing so can we 【美好愿景】.

三、功能句型库（真题原句 + 槽位，按功能分 9 类）

1️⃣ 引出现象 / 观点（P1、P2 开头）

句型 真题原句
There is a growing awareness that... There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world.（2024.6）
People are now increasingly aware of... People are now increasingly aware of the danger of 'appearance anxiety'.（2023.3）
more and more people have come to realize... more and more college students have come to realize social practice and academic learning are equally important.（2024.6）
It is (now) widely accepted/believed that... It is believed that teachers can exert a profound influence on their students' academic pursuit and personal development.（2025.12）
With the development/application of..., ... With the increasing application of AI technology, there is a growing concern that...（2025.6）
In an era/age of..., it is vitally important to... In an era of information explosion, it is vitally important to develop the ability to think critically.（2022.12）

2️⃣ 陈述理由（P2）

  • There are two compelling/solid/convincing reasons why this ability matters.（2022.12 / 2022.9 / 2022.12）
  • The most obvious reason lies in the speed of technological change.（2022.6）→ 槽位：The most obvious reason lies in 【名词短语】.
  • The reason is straightforward/simple.（2025.6 / 2022.9）
  • The reason is not hard to understand.（2026.6）
  • ... matters far more than simply filling their heads with facts, because...（2023.6）
  • whether we are selecting a course, a product or a career, rational judgement based on reliable evidence saves us from regret.（2022.12）→ 槽位：whether we are 【doing A】, 【B】 or 【C】, ...

3️⃣ 对比论证（P2 神器，六级范文出现 10+ 次）

  • Those who can teach themselves new tools quickly adapt to changing workplaces, while those who wait to be taught inevitably fall behind.（2024.6）→ 槽位：Those who 【做A的人】..., while those who 【不做A的人】...
  • not because they were less clever, but because they lacked the digital skills the new setting demanded.（2024.6）→ not because..., but because...
  • children grow strongest not when every step is arranged for them, but when they are trusted to try, fail, and try again.（2023.12）→ not when..., but when...
  • Small as these acts seem, they add up to a sustainable way of living.（2022.6）→ 让步倒装：形容词/名词 + as + 主谓, ...
  • Far from being a burden, seniors possess a lifetime of professional knowledge.（2023.12）→ Far from being..., ... 远非…而是…

4️⃣ 举例（P2 必备，8 种写法轮换）

写法 真题原句
Take my own experience: ... Take my own experience: when our university suddenly shifted to online courses, I taught myself data analysis.（2024.6）
Take sb as an example Take my cousin as an example: instead of chasing a comfortable office job, she founded a start-up...（2025.12）
A case in point is... A case in point is my own experience: last year I nearly forwarded a false rumour...（2025.6）
A telling example is... A telling example is my own experience in a group research project last term.（2022.9）
In my own case, ... In my own case, learning to use spreadsheets turned a painful week into an afternoon's work.（2022.9）
I still remember... I still remember one winter when my classmates and I spent a weekend teaching migrant children.（2022.6）
My own experience convinces me that... Her experience convinces me that continuous learning is what keeps a person competitive.（2022.6）
That experience taught me that... That experience taught me that challenges are conquered by those who keep improving.（2025.12）

5️⃣ 因果 / 方式（P2 论证推进）

  • By questioning the source, comparing different views and weighing the evidence, we can turn the endless flood of information into a real advantage rather than a heavy burden.（2022.12）→ 槽位：By doing A, B and C, we can turn X into Y rather than Z.（黄金句型）
  • By narrowing the field in advance, I turn an overwhelming list into a manageable shortlist.（2023.3）
  • Thanks to AI-powered tutors, questions that once took days to solve can be answered in seconds.（2022.12）
  • As a result, we save time and study with greater focus.（2022.12）

6️⃣ 升级句式（阅卷老师眼前一亮的 8 种）

句式 真题原句 出处
强调句 It is...that... it is teamwork that turns scattered abilities into real strength 2022.12
强调句变体 it is perseverance and daily self-control that truly shape our future 2024.12
Only + 状语倒装 Only by standing on such firm ground can we climb steadily toward true mastery. 2023.12
Only when...倒装 Only when we dare to be honest can genuine collaboration take root and bear fruit. 2022.9
Only then/after 倒装 Only after he sought counseling did he truly recover. 2023.6
分词开头 Bombarded daily by filtered images and narrow aesthetic standards, many young people measure themselves against unreachable models. 2023.3
which 非限制性定从 ..., which makes the capacity to learn on our own a decisive advantage. 2024.6
The more..., the more... The deeper we understand traditional Chinese culture, the more confident we become in facing the world. 2026.6

再加三种进阶：

  • 虚拟语气：Had I shared it, I would have misled hundreds of classmates.（2025.6）→ Had I done..., I would have...
  • It is high time that + 过去式：It is high time that college students stopped drifting and started planning ahead.（2025.12）
  • what 主语从句：what my teachers really gave me was not the code, but the confidence and method to explore an unfamiliar field by myself.（2023.6）

7️⃣ 类比 / 比喻（升华专用，背 5 个）

  • a lifelong engine that drives personal growth（2024.6）终身引擎
  • cooperation is built on sand（2022.9）建立在沙滩上（不牢靠）
  • mental and physical health are two wheels of the same cart（2023.6 / 2024.6）一体两轮
  • Creativity is a muscle that strengthens only through active practice.（2025.6）创造力是肌肉
  • Each small success became a brick building his confidence.（2023.12）成功是砖
  • a bridge to a wider future（2022.6）通往更宽未来的桥
  • when opportunity knocks, they are ready to open the door（2025.6）机会敲门

8️⃣ "远不止…更是…"结尾升华三件套

  • ... is far more than an academic skill; it is a lifelong engine that drives personal growth.（2024.6）
  • ... is not an optional skill but a survival tool for the modern mind.（2022.12）
  • ... is not only a personal virtue but also a social necessity.（2026.6）
  • 扩充：... is no longer a luxury but a basic survival skill. / ... is not a cost but an investment.

9️⃣ 结尾行动倡议（P3 最后一句，倒装收尾最稳）

  • Therefore, we should consciously develop this ability and treat every day as a chance to learn.（2024.6）
  • I firmly believe that whoever embraces new skills today will stand firm tomorrow.（2022.6）
  • If each of us is willing to reach out to those in need, our society will surely grow kinder, warmer and more united.（2022.6）→ 槽位：If each of us ..., our society will surely grow 【三个比较级】.
  • Only by integrating nature into urban life can residents truly develop a stronger love for it.（2026.6）
  • Let us step out of the virtual world more often and reconnect with the people around us.（2022.9）

四、话题素材库（理由段直接抄）

🎓 教育学习类

  • 理由：知识更新快 → Knowledge updates at an unprecedented pace / Skills that guaranteed a stable job ten years ago may become outdated overnight（2022.6）
  • 理由：知识会变，能力不变 → knowledge itself keeps changing, while the skill of learning does not（2023.6）
  • 理由：基础决定高度 → Just as a towering building cannot stand firm without a solid foundation, no student can reach academic heights unless the fundamentals are first laid down.（2023.12）
  • 理由：理论需实践检验 → Knowledge that never leaves the textbook easily fades, yet once applied, it truly takes root.（2024.6）
  • 例子：自学数据分析、线上课程转型、选修课转专业、室友自律拿国奖

📱 科技社会类

  • 效率：at the click of a button 一键搞定 / questions that once took days to solve can be answered in seconds（2022.12）
  • 信息泛滥：we are flooded with news, advertisements and social-media posts, yet not all of them are trustworthy（2022.12）
  • 责任使用：verify information before sharing it / a single careless post can spread misinformation within minutes（2025.6）
  • AI 边界：AI should serve as a helpful assistant rather than a replacement for our own minds.（2025.6）
  • 数字鸿沟：many senior citizens find themselves left behind, struggling to keep pace with tools that younger generations take for granted（2023.3）→ 解决：families ought to patiently teach their parents digital skills, while service providers should retain user-friendly alternatives（2023.3）

🌱 品质成长类

  • 自律：Rather than blaming the environment for our failures, we should learn to say no to temptations and stick to our own plans.（2024.12）
  • 目标+坚持：by aiming first at three kilometres, then five, and finally the full distance, the runner sets goals within reach and steadily raises the bar. Each small victory fuels the next.（2024.12）
  • 团队：almost every modern task demands that people with different strengths pool their efforts; without cooperation, even talented individuals can accomplish little（2022.12）
  • 信任：trust removes the fear that keeps partners guarding themselves, while openness lets them pool their strengths toward a common goal（2022.9）
  • 分歧：Rather than shouting others down or stubbornly clinging to our own views, seeking common ground through calm reasoning is always the wiser choice.（2023.6）
  • 心理健康：a troubled mind can be just as disabling as a broken leg（2023.6）
  • 文化自信：cultural confidence cannot be borrowed from outside; it must grow from real knowledge of what their ancestors created.（2026.6）

💼 职业发展类

  • 就业竞争：employers demand not only a diploma but also practical competence（2025.6）→ employers value candidates who can communicate, cooperate and solve real problems（2025.12）
  • 提前准备：waiting until graduation to think about work is simply too late（2025.6）
  • 例子套话：stand out in an internship interview / landed an internship at a tech firm / turned the pressure of competition into a driving force（2025.12）
  • 老年人价值：age brings not decline but a rich resource waiting to be tapped（2023.12）

二、五大主题词汇表（真题原文 + 扩充）

🏛 1. 名胜与建筑

位置/地位

  • be located in + 地点 位于 | The CCTV Headquarters is located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing.
  • Located 15 kilometres southwest of..., ... 位于…西南15公里处（分词开头，高级）
  • span the river 横跨河流 | spanning the Yongding River
  • the oldest existing multi-arch stone bridge 现存最古老的多拱石桥
  • one of the famous tourist attractions 著名景点之一
  • become a popular attraction in the city 成为热门景点
  • be known as "the Roof of the World" 被称为"世界屋脊"
  • be regarded as a monument in the history of... 被公认为…史上的一座丰碑

建造/结构

  • be built in the Sui Dynasty (around 605 AD) 建于隋朝
  • be rebuilt in 1698 重建 | be supported by 281 pillars 由281根柱子支撑
  • be designed by China and built with domestic materials 中国设计、国产材料建造
  • consist of / Consisting of two towers 由两座塔楼组成
  • Its upper deck, a four-lane highway, is 4,589 meters long 上层为四车道公路桥（同位语插入）
  • a total floor area of about 550,000 square meters 总建筑面积约55万平米
  • with a length of 50.82 meters, a width of 9.6 meters and a span of 37.37 meters 长/宽/跨度

价值/意义

  • mark a great leap in China's bridge construction 标志着…的一个飞跃
  • be recognized as / be acknowledged as 被公认为
  • innovative structure 创新结构 | be the result of long-term cooperation between... 是…长期合作的成果
  • attract crowds of visitors 吸引众多游客 | enjoy spectacular views of... 欣赏…壮观景色
  • 扩充：historic site 历史遗址 / architectural marvel 建筑奇观 / exquisite craftsmanship 精湛工艺 / integrate...with... 将…与…融合

习俗/节日

  • an important custom for Chinese people 中国人的重要习俗
  • pasting Spring Festival couplets 贴春联 | a four-character horizontal scroll 四字横批

三、翻译核心句型 21 式（公式 + 真题例句）

🔧 A. 句子骨架类

① 分词状语开头（地理/名胜万能开头，33 套里出现 15+ 次）

公式：Located in/位于 + 地点, 主语 + 是…

  • Located 15 kilometres southwest of Tian'anmen Square and spanning the Yongding River, Lugou Bridge is the oldest existing multi-arch stone bridge in Beijing.
  • Located in the southwest of China, the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau... is known as "the Roof of the World".
  • Located mostly in Yunnan Province and Guizhou Province, the Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau... is the fourth largest plateau in China.

② with 复合结构带数据（面积/海拔/长宽）

公式：主语, with an area of... and an average altitude of..., is...

  • the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, with an area of about 2.3 million square kilometers and an average altitude of over 4,000 meters, is known as...
  • The Zhaozhou Bridge was built in the Sui Dynasty, around 605 AD, with a length of 50.82 meters, a width of 9.6 meters and a span of 37.37 meters.
  • 变体：boasts over 110 cubic meters of usable space and is able to accommodate three astronauts, orbiting at an altitude of...

③ 「由…组成」

  • A Spring Festival couplet consists of a pair of poetic lines and a four-character horizontal scroll.
  • Consisting of two towers, the main building has become a popular attraction.
  • The main building is made up of two towers.（扩充写法）

④ 「是…之一 / 最…」

  • one of the famous tourist attractions in Nanjing
  • one of the largest deep-water ports in the world
  • the first road-rail bridge over the Yangtze River that was designed by China（the first...that 定从）
  • making China one of the few countries in the world that independently possess a global satellite navigation system

⑤ 「被誉为/被称为/被视为」五连

  • be known as "the Roof of the World"
  • be regarded as the finest works of stone carving / be regarded as a symbol of the character of the Chinese nation
  • be seen as / be considered (to be) / be honored as
  • has long been cherished by the Chinese people 一直以来深受喜爱

⑥ 「不仅…而且…」（几乎每篇都有）

  • Lugou Bridge is not only world-famous for its aesthetic features but also widely recognized as a monument...
  • it not only reflects environmental awareness but also greatly saves construction materials
  • This not only marks China's tremendous progress in aerospace technology, but also demonstrates that...（收尾神句）

🔧 B. 动作表达类

⑦ 「对…起到作用/做出贡献」

  • played a huge role in promoting economic development and improving people's lives
  • played a very positive role in promoting economic and cultural exchanges
  • made great contributions to maintaining regional peace / to the progress of human civilization
  • make greater contributions to building Shanghai into a global shipping center

⑧ 「促进/推动/提升」动词链

  • promote economic development / promote regional economic development
  • boost / facilitate / advance / enhance / markedly improve
  • greatly facilitated the exchange of goods and the movement of people 大大方便了物资交流和人员来往

⑨ 「使/让」兼语 → enable / make / allow

  • enables doctors to quickly review patients' medical histories, thus avoiding repeated examinations
  • enables urban consumers to interact with sellers in real time
  • make cities more beautiful, safer and more livable（无宾补结构）
  • which has not only markedly improved... but also made the cities more beautiful

⑩ 动作串联：分词/定从降级（中文几个动词连排 → 英文一主几辅）

  • Travelling mainly on foot, he visited many remote and isolated areas.
  • Braving dangers and overcoming numerous difficulties, he made two journeys..., opened up trade..., transported... and thereby pioneered...
  • The railway bridge connects the two former railway lines..., reducing the time from one and a half hours in the past to just two minutes now.（reducing 结果状语）

⑪ 「随着…」两种写法

  • With + 名词：With the rapid development of China's economy and the steady improvement of people's living standards, ... / With the continuous increase in government and social investment, ... / With the deepening of the reform and opening up, ...
  • As + 从句：As the Internet and logistics networks rapidly extend into rural areas, ...

⑫ 「因此/从而」显化逻辑

  • so that more elderly people can benefit（so that 目的/结果）
  • thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance / thereby improving the accuracy of diagnosis（thus/thereby + doing）
  • which help discharge floodwater, reduce the weight of the bridge and save stone（which 从句装三个动作）

⑬ 「无论…都」

  • no matter how harsh the environment is, it can survive tenaciously

⑭ 「自古/自从…以来」

  • Since ancient times, the seal has been a proof of identity and a symbol of power in China.
  • Since its completion, the bridge has withstood many floods and earthquakes, but its main structure remains intact.
  • Fans have long been cherished by the Chinese people.
  • Exploring space has always been a dream of the Chinese nation.（always + 现在完成时）

⑮ 「若没有…就不可能」虚拟语气（历史人物篇收尾）

  • without Zhang Qian's mission to the Western Regions, there would have been no opening of the Silk Road, nor any cultural exchanges...

⑯ 「正如…所指出的」

  • As historians have pointed out, ...

🔧 C. 无主句/被动句（中文没有主语时的 4 种出路）

中文「兴建了…」「开设了…」「被定为…」高频无主句，处理方案：

方案 1：直接被动

  • Various elderly care institutions have been built across the country. 全国兴建了各类养老机构
  • July 11... has been designated as China's Maritime Day. 被定为航海节
  • Drones have been included by the state in strategic emerging industries. 被国家纳入…
  • many objects wishing the couple happiness are placed around it 摆放着许多物件

方案 2：The government / China 作主语（主动化）

  • The Chinese government has introduced a series of policies to encourage and support...
  • the government has issued a series of standards / the government keeps increasing social security funding

方案 3：There be / 转主语

  • there is a popular saying in the Beijing area: "..." 有…的说法

方案 4：地区/事物作主语

  • many cities have opened community canteens / hospitals shift appointment booking online

🔧 D. 收尾/展望类

⑰ 「为…注入活力/动力」

  • injecting unprecedented vitality into China's rural development
  • injecting new momentum into economic and social development
  • The spirit of self-reliance has become a powerful driving force behind the country's modernization

⑱ 「未来将更…」展望句

  • Yangshan Port will continue to develop and make greater contributions to...
  • In the future, China will take steadier and further steps in its exploration of space.
  • the elderly will live a more convenient, comfortable, healthy and happy life（四形容词连排）
  • drones will be able to realize their unique value in more fields

⑲ 「A 与 B 相辅相成」

  • personal ambition and national progress reinforce each other
  • Learning guided my practice, and practice enriched my learning.（写作也适用）

⑳ 「造福/惠及」

  • telemedicine has made high-quality medical resources accessible to more patients
  • it is expected to benefit more than 400 million people
  • provide reliable water resources for people in the region

㉑ 数字换算表（必背，错一个全段扣分）

中文 英文 例

亿 ×100,000,000 → hundred million 4亿 → 400 million

四、汉英差异四大招（翻译执行的操作系统）

招式 1：先拆句，再动笔

中文一逗到底，英文按语义断句。切分标准：中文句号/分号优先，主语转换处次之。

  • 例（卢沟桥第1句）：「位于…，横跨…，是…」三节 → 英文把「位于+横跨」降级为分词状语，只留「是」作主干。

招式 2：找主干，降修饰

每句先问「谁是主语、哪个动词最核心」，其余全部降级为：分词短语（doing/done）、with 复合结构、定语从句（which/that/who）、同位语（a four-lane highway）、介词短语。

  • 例（南京大桥）：「上层的4车道公路桥长4589米」→ Its upper deck, a four-lane highway, is 4,589 meters long（同位语插入）。

招式 3：显化逻辑

中文靠语序暗示因果/递进，英文要用连词/分词明示：thus、thereby、so that、which、and thereby、due to。

  • 例（竹子）：「生命力强…因而寓意坚韧」→ bamboo has strong vitality and adaptability; no matter how harsh the environment is, it can survive tenaciously, thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance.

招式 4：代词承接，避免重复

专有名词出现 2 次后改用代词或换称。

  • 例（卢沟桥）：第 1 句 Lugou Bridge → 第 2 句用 It was first built...（不再重复桥名）
  • 例（婚礼）：第 3 句换角度用 The wedding venue 切入，不重复 wedding celebration。
