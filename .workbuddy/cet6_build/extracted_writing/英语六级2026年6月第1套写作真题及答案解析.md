真题卷第1套(2026-06) • 英语六级 Part I

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence "With the trend of Urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for Nature." You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your

essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words. You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay.

【参考范文 · 164 词】 写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

With the trend of Urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for

Nature. As concrete forests expand, many urban dwellers rarely feel the breeze, hear birds, or touch soil. A

stronger bond with nature is not a luxury; it is essential for mental and physical well-being in an increasingly

artificial environment.

One practical way to cultivate this love is to make nature part of daily routines. For example, residents can

walk in nearby parks, grow plants on balconies, or join community gardening projects. These activities

reconnect people with the living world. Last year, I started visiting a small riverbank park every weekend.

Watching seasons change there reduced my stress and made me more aware of environmental protection.

Therefore, city governments and individuals should work together to create more green spaces and spend time

in them. Only by integrating nature into urban life can residents truly develop a stronger love for it and build

healthier cities for future generations.

中心立意

城市居民应把亲近自然变成日常，以缓解城市化带来的疏离

以 With the trend of Urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a

stronger love for Nature. 为出发点，中心论点是：面对城市化，居民需要通过日常行动主动重 建与自然的联系。

要展开的要点

1. 城市化让居民与自然渐行渐远

描述城市扩张带来的'水泥森林'现象，说明为什么增强爱自然之心变得迫切。

2. 日常亲近自然是培养热爱的有效途径

用具体例子或个人经历说明散步、养花、社区园艺等活动如何让居民重新感受自然。

3. 政府与个人应共建绿色空间

从社会层面提出对策：增加绿地并鼓励居民使用，把热爱自然落实到城市生活中。

漏写或改写开头句

反面示例：With the development of urbanization, city people should love nature more. 问题：改

写了原句。修正：With the trend of Urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to

develop a stronger love for Nature.

脱离城市化泛泛谈环保

反面示例：We should protect forests and save wild animals. 问题：没有城市背景，不符合题意。

修正：Urban residents can start by visiting city parks and growing plants on balconies.

只评论不举例

反面示例：Nature is very important. We must love it. 问题：无具体例证，缺乏说服力。修正：For

example, walking in a nearby park every weekend helps me feel relaxed and closer to nature.

字数不足或超标

反面示例：全文仅120词。问题：未达 You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200

words. 的硬要求。修正：增加个人经历与具体做法，使内容充实到150词以上。

堆砌万能模板

反面示例：With the development of society, nature is becoming more and more important. 问

题：套话空洞，未回应城市化背景。修正：As concrete forests expand, city residents need to

rebuild their connection with nature.
