真题卷第2套(2024-12) • 英语六级 Part I

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence "Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their

personal growth." You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay.

【参考范文 · 164 词】 写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. In

an age flooded with short videos and endless distractions, the ability to control oneself has become the very foundation of academic success and character building.

A vivid example is my roommate Li Ming. When we entered university, he set himself a firm rule: no phone

before finishing the day's study plan. While others drifted from one game to another, his steady self

management allowed him to master a difficult programming course and win a national scholarship. His

experience convinced me that talent alone rarely decides how far a person goes; it is perseverance and daily

self-control that truly shape our future.

Therefore, I firmly believe that cultivating self-discipline deserves our full attention. Rather than blaming the

environment for our failures, we should learn to say no to temptations and stick to our own plans. Only by

mastering ourselves can we grow into the people we truly wish to become.

读者 · 语域

面向阅卷人的正式书面语

本题没有具体收信人，属纯议论大作文，读者即阅卷老师，用正式书面语即可，不写称呼、不

落款、不署真名。唯一的格式硬点是首句地位——You should copy the sentence given in

quotes at the beginning of your essay.，它必须原样出现在文章最开头，而不是埋在段中当过 渡句。

中心立意

自律是成长的根基

全篇中心一句话：自律是学业成功与品格养成的根基，尤其在充满诱惑的信息时代，能否管住

自己往往决定一个人能走多远。这正是对首句 Nowadays, more and more students have

realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. 的顺承与坐实。

要展开的要点

1. 时代背景下自律更稀缺可贵

短视频、游戏等诱惑遍地，能控制自己的能力反而成了稀缺品，从背景角度点明自律为何值得

重视。

2. 具体例子证明自律决定成败

用一个身边人或亲身经历（如室友定规矩、坚持学习最终拿奖学金），落实 cite examples or

use your personal experiences to develop your essay，证明决定成败的是自律而非天赋。

3. 呼吁拒绝诱惑坚持计划

结尾把中心推向行动：与其抱怨环境，不如学会对诱惑说不、坚持自己的计划，掌控自己才能

成长。

第 1 段 照抄首句并点题

原样照抄 Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self

第 3 段 总结升华呼吁

第 2 段 室友定规矩坚持终获奖学金

写室友立下『学习计划没完成不碰手机』的规矩，别人沉迷游戏时他靠 self-management 攻

下难课、拿到国家奖学金，由此得出：决定人走多远的是 perseverance 与自律，而非天赋。

引出例证 · A vivid example is \_\_\_. When \_\_\_, he/she set himself/herself a firm rule:

\_\_\_.（一句点名例子并交代背景）

对比反衬 · While others \_\_\_, his/her steady \_\_\_ allowed him/her to \_\_\_.（用别人反

衬主角的自律成效）

由例回扣中心 · His/Her experience convinced me that \_\_\_; it is \_\_\_ that truly \_\_\_.

（把例子拉回论点，防止叙议脱节）

第 3 段 重申＋拒绝诱惑坚持计划＋升华

重申自律值得全力重视，建议与其把失败归咎环境，不如对诱惑说不、坚持自己的计划，升

华到『唯有掌控自己，才能成为想成为的人』，呼应首句。

重申立场 · Therefore, I firmly believe that \_\_\_ deserves our full attention.（一句回扣

中心，不引新论点）

给建议对策 · Rather than \_\_\_, we should learn to \_\_\_ and \_\_\_.（把道理落到具体行

动）

升华呼应 · Only by \_\_\_ can we \_\_\_.（用倒装收尾，升华并呼应首句）

内容切题 紧扣自律并用例子展开

本题必须始终围绕『自律对成长的重要性』，且用上 You can make comments, cite

examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. 中的方式；最易 失分处是写着写着跑去谈『努力』『时间管理』等相邻话题，偏离自律主线。

结构连贯 三段清晰首尾呼应

本题达标要点是首段提出、中段证明、尾段收结分工清楚，且结尾 Only by

mastering ourselves 呼应首句形成闭环；易失分处是通篇不分段或例子与结论之间缺 过渡。

语言准确 自律同义替换＋句式多样

本题应把 self-discipline 换用 self-control、self-management、perseverance 等避免

全篇重复；六级层面还应有一两处像 Only by mastering ourselves can we grow 的倒

装或从句提升语言分，但须确保正确、不为炫技牺牲准确。

字数格式 控词数并原样照抄首句

本题硬约束是 You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words. 且

须 copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay；最致命的失分

是漏抄或改写首句，其次是为凑字数注水或超出上限。

典型低分写法 · 反面示例与改法

改写首句直接扣分

反面示例（改写了首句）：These days, students increasingly think that self-control matters a lot

for growing up.——这是改写、不是照抄，直接丢掉硬性分。正确做法是原样搬：Nowadays, more

and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. 放在开 头一字不改。

空喊口号论证空洞

反面示例（空洞）：Self-discipline is very important. Everyone should have self-discipline. Without

it, no one can succeed.——三句同义反复、无支撑。改成有细节的：My roommate refused to

touch his phone until his daily study plan was finished, and this habit helped him win a

scholarship.

同义重复凑字数

反面示例（注水）：Self-discipline is important. It is really important. We all know it is important

for us.——三句没增加任何信息。改成推进式：Self-discipline is important because it turns vague

goals into daily action, which is exactly what most students lack.

全篇重复一个词

反面示例（重复）：Self-discipline helps us study. Self-discipline helps us grow. Self-discipline

decides our future.——通篇 self-discipline。改成轮换：Self-control helps us study, self

management helps us grow, and perseverance ultimately decides our future.
