真题卷第3套(2024-06) • 英语六级 Part I

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today’s world.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You

should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words. (You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay. )

【参考范文 · 170 词】 写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today’s world. As study, work

and daily life move online, the ability to use digital tools wisely has become a basic survival skill rather than an

optional advantage.

This importance is easy to feel in real situations. During the pandemic, all my courses were shifted to online

platforms, and only classmates who could navigate video meetings, submit assignments and search for reliable

sources kept up with the pace. Those who struggled with the technology quickly fell behind, not because they

were less clever, but because they lacked the digital skills the new setting demanded. The same gap appears in

the job market, where employers now expect graduates to handle data, communicate online and tell trustworthy

information from rumours.

Therefore, digital literacy is no longer a bonus but a necessity. By actively learning to process information,

protect our online safety and keep updating our skills, we can turn technology into a real advantage instead of

a barrier.

读者 · 语域

面向阅卷人的正式书面语

本题是纯议论大作文，没有具体收信人，所以不设称呼、不落款，语域按正式书面英语走即

可：不用缩写口语（don't 写成 do not）、不用第二人称直接教训读者。展开方式题目给了自

由度——You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop

your essay.，可评论、举例或写个人经历，但语气都要保持客观得体。

中心立意

数字素养已是生存必备

本篇中心：在学习、工作、生活全面上网的今天，数字素养与技能已从「加分项」变成「生存

必备」。这条立场直接顺着给定句 There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital

literacy and skills in today’s world. 往前推一步，既贴题又给后文留出了论证空间。

要展开的要点

1. 网课场景见真章

用一个具体场景落地：疫情期间课程全搬到线上，会用视频会议、交作业、查可靠资料的同学 跟得上，不会的很快掉队——直接呼应 use your personal experiences to develop your essay 的个人经历路径。

2. 求职市场的门槛

从个人推到社会：如今用人单位默认毕业生能处理数据、线上沟通、辨别真假信息，数字技能 成了求职硬门槛，说明重要性不止在校园。

3. 主动学习是出路

结尾给对策：既然重要，就要主动学会信息处理、保护上网安全、持续更新技能，把技术变成

优势而非障碍，收束回中心。

第 1 段 抄首句＋亮中心

原样照抄 There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in

第 2 段 场景论证重要性

主体段：用网课亲历＋求职门槛两个具体场景，正反对照证明数字素养为什么重要。

举例过渡 · This importance is easy to feel in real situations. During \_\_\_, only those

内容切题 全篇扣紧重要性

本题中心是「数字素养的重要性」，三段都得扣住它：别写成介绍数字工具有哪些

（跑题），也别只抄首句却不表态。题目允许 make comments, cite examples or use

your personal experiences，选其一写透即可。

结构连贯 三段路标清晰

本范文用 This importance is easy to feel（引例）、not because...but because（对

照）、Therefore（收尾）三处路标把三段逻辑串起来。主体考生易犯的是通篇不分

段或段间无过渡，务必让每段功能一眼可辨。

语言准确 数字素养话题词

本题可稳用一批话题表达：digital literacy、process information、online safety、

reliable sources、keep updating skills。这些词既贴题又不易写错，比生造的中式长 句稳妥。

字数格式 卡住硬区间

本题字数是硬区间：You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200

words.，两头都扣分。篇幅只够一个饱满主体段，别贪多铺开三四个论点导致每点都 浅。

典型低分写法 · 反面示例与改法

改写首句失分

反面示例：把开头写成「Nowadays people gradually realize digital skills are important.」——这是

改写、不是照抄，copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay 的要求没满

足。改法：老老实实抄「There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and

skills in today’s world.」再往下写。

通篇空喊无支撑

反面示例：「Digital literacy is very important. Everyone thinks it is important. Without it, life is

very hard.」全是形容词、零场景。改法：换成一个具体情境——「During online classes, students

who could not use digital tools quickly fell behind.」用事实说话。
